Eight pronunciations, eight perfect scores

'Bajate en Callao y caminá dos cuadras' is the canonical test of the Rioplatense [ʃ]: the phrase set marks it as unmeasurable by alignment because the phoneme is intervocalic. 'Fuimos de Ushuaia a Neuquén en auto' is measured automatically and every model scores WER 0.000 on it. Four models, two phrases, eight pronunciations, eight perfect scores. This is where the dialect question lives in v0.
